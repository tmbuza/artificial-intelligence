# AI-001 — One-page problem brief

- **Task name and version:**
- **Author / review date:**
- **Direct user and current workflow:**
- **People affected by the output:**
- **Problem evidence:** What observation shows a need? What is still unknown?
- **Unit of work:** What does one input/output pair represent?
- **Permitted inputs and approved sources:** Include ownership and update responsibility.
- **Output contract:** Required fields, evidence, and allowed values.
- **Intended action:** What happens after the output is checked?
- **Out of scope:** Tasks, information, and actions excluded from this version.
- **Baseline:** Current process or simple method; explain the comparison.
- **Development cases:** Routine, ambiguous, and outside-scope cases with labels.
- **Independent evaluation plan:** Who labels cases, and how are they held aside?
- **Error measures:** Definitions, denominators, and error consequences.
- **Workload measure:** Unit and collection method, including correction time.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Numerical or categorical criteria agreed before comparison.
- **Human review:** Who checks what evidence, when, and with what authority?
- **Referral conditions:** At least three conditions and the responsible person.
- **Constraints:** Language, accessibility, privacy, latency, cost, and available time.
- **Next experiment and stopping condition:** What uncertainty will it resolve?

## Framing statement

For [user], given [permitted input], produce [defined output] to support
[task/action]. Compare with [baseline] using [cases and criteria]. Refer
[conditions] to [person], within [constraints].
